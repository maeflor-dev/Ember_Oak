Our Story

Crafted by hand,
guided by origin.

Ember & Oak began with a simple obsession: to understand what makes a great cup of coffee. We travel to farms across Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala, meeting the growers who make it possible.

Every batch is roasted on-site in our vintage Probat, then rested and brewed to order. No shortcuts — just patience, precision and respect for the bean.

"Every batch is roasted on-site. No shortcuts."
  • Direct-trade relationships with smallholder farms
  • Small-batch roasting on a vintage Probat
  • Q-grader certified baristas on every shift
